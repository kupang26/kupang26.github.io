Frequently asked questions

How do I track my sleep?

Tap the moon button in the middle of the tab bar, then place your iPhone near your bed (plugged in is recommended). The App listens for sounds such as snoring or talking while you sleep and shows a summary in the morning.

The App says it can't use the microphone.

Open the iPhone Settings app, find Sleep Tracker, and turn on Microphone. Sleep tracking needs the microphone to measure noise.

My alarm didn't ring.

Make sure notifications are allowed for Sleep Tracker in Settings, your ringer volume is up, and Focus / Do Not Disturb is not silencing the App. Keep the App open or running in the background while you sleep for the most reliable alarm.

Are my recordings uploaded anywhere?

No. Recordings and sleep data are stored only on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the App removes all of its data, including recordings and sleep history, from your device.

Is Sleep Tracker a medical device?

No. The App is for general wellness only. If you have concerns about your sleep, please talk to a healthcare professional.
