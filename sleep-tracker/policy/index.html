Privacy Policy

Sleep Tracker · Kupang Studio · Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sleep Tracker app ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. We built the App so that your data stays on your device. We do not run servers that collect your personal data, and we do not sell or share your data.

1. Information the App uses

Microphone and sleep sounds

With your permission, the App uses the microphone while sleep tracking is active to measure ambient noise and detect sound events (such as snoring or talking). Short audio clips of these events may be saved only on your device so you can listen to them later. Audio is processed locally and is never uploaded to us or to any third party.

Camera and photo library

If you choose to set a profile photo, the App asks for access to your camera or photo library. The selected image is stored only on your device and is used only to display your profile.

Sleep data and settings

Sleep sessions, alarms, bedtime reminders, and preferences you enter are stored locally on your device in the App's private storage.

Notifications

The App uses local notifications for alarms and bedtime reminders. These are scheduled on your device and do not involve sending your data anywhere.

2. What we do not collect

  • We do not require an account or collect your name, email, or contact details.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.

3. Data storage and deletion

All data remains on your device and may be included in your device's own backups (for example, iCloud Backup) according to your Apple settings. You can delete all App data, including recordings and sleep records, at any time by uninstalling the App.

4. Permissions

You can grant or revoke microphone, camera, photo library, and notification permissions at any time in your device's Settings. Some features may not work without the related permission.

5. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
