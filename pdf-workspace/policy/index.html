Privacy Policy

PDF Workspace · Kupang Studio · Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the PDF Workspace app ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. The App is designed so that your documents stay on your device. We do not run servers that receive your documents or personal data, and we do not sell or share your data.

1. Your documents

PDFs and images you import, documents you scan, and files created with the App's tools (such as merged, split, or compressed PDFs) are stored only on your device, in the App's private storage. The App does not upload your documents to us or to any third party.

When you open a password-protected PDF, the password is used only to open the document while it is on screen. It is not saved.

2. Information the App uses

Camera (document scanning)

With your permission, the App uses your camera only while you are scanning a document, through Apple's built-in document scanner. The scanned pages are processed on your device and are saved only when you choose to save them.

Photos

When you add photos, the App uses the system photo picker. The App only receives the photos you select and does not have access to the rest of your photo library.

Files

When you import from the Files app, the App only accesses the files you choose. It copies them into its own storage; it does not change the originals.

Text recognition and search

Text recognition (OCR) is performed on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Recognized text is saved inside your PDF, and a search index of your documents' text is kept in the App's private storage on your device so you can search your library. Neither is uploaded.

Settings

The App stores a few preferences on your device, such as your chosen theme, language, reading layout, the last page you read in each document, and how your library is sorted.

Free-use counters

Some features can be used a limited number of times without Premium. The App counts how many free uses you have made of each such feature and stores these numbers in your device's Keychain, so they remain if you reinstall the App. The counters contain no information about your documents and are never sent anywhere.

Editing and signatures

Edits you make in the App (highlights, drawings, text, notes, signatures and page changes) are saved only into your documents on your device. Signatures you create are stored as drawings in the App's private storage on your device, protected by your device's data protection and excluded from device backups. They are never uploaded, and you can delete a saved signature at any time in the App.

3. Printing and sharing

When you print, the document is sent by your device directly to the printer you select (for example, over AirPrint on your local network). When you use the share sheet, the document is sent only to the destination you choose, such as Mail, AirDrop, or Files. These actions happen only when you start them. Apps and services you share with are governed by their own privacy policies.

Copies prepared for sharing or printing are temporary and are removed by the App afterwards.

4. Purchases

Premium subscriptions are purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment; we do not receive your payment details. The App only checks with Apple, on your device, whether your subscription is active. Apple's handling of your purchase is described in Apple's privacy policy.

5. What we do not collect

  • We do not require an account or collect your name, email, or contact details.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.

6. Data storage and deletion

Your documents and App data remain on your device and may be included in your device's own backups (for example, iCloud Backup) according to your Apple settings. You can delete a document in the App at any time, which also removes it from the App's search index. Uninstalling the App deletes all of its data from your device, including saved signatures.

7. Permissions

You can grant or revoke camera access at any time in your device's Settings. Scanning is not available without camera access; the rest of the App continues to work.

8. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. If a future version of the App adds features that send data off your device (for example, cloud sync or AI features), this policy will be updated before those features are released, and they will require your clear consent.
