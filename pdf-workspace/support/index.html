Frequently asked questions

How do I scan a document?

Open the Scan tab and tap Start Scanning. Hold your iPhone over the page; edges are found automatically. Add more pages, then tap Save to Library.

The App says camera access is off.

Open the iPhone Settings app, find PDF Workspace, and turn on Camera.

How do I import PDFs or photos?

In the Library tab, tap + and choose Import from Files or Import from Photos.

How do I sign a document?

Open the document, tap Edit, then Sign. Create a signature once, choose it, and tap the page where it should go. Drag it to move it, then tap Save.

Where are my signatures stored? How do I delete one?

Signatures are stored only on your iPhone and are never uploaded. In the Editor's Sign mode, touch and hold a saved signature and choose Delete Signature.

How do free uses work?

Scanning, merging, splitting, images to PDF, printing and editing each include 5 free uses. A use is counted only when the action completes. Free uses do not renew. You can see what's left in Settings › Free uses left. Premium includes unlimited use of every feature.

I subscribed on another device or reinstalled the App.

Open the Premium screen (Settings › Get Premium) and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings › Manage Subscription in the App, or the iPhone Settings app › your name › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Search doesn't find words in my scan.

Scanned pages are pictures until text recognition runs. Open the document and tap Recognize Text (or turn on Make text searchable when saving a scan). Text recognition is part of Premium.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings › Language. The App switches immediately; there's no need to restart.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. Documents, recognized text, and signatures stay on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Delete documents in the Library, or uninstall the App to remove all of its data, including saved signatures, from your device.
